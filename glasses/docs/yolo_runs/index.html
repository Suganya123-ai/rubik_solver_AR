YOLO training runs

Model: yolo26n-pose (2.55 M parameters), finding one "cube" box plus its 7 corner points. Trained on 2,500 synthetic corner-view frames, validated on 300 more. Planned for 30 passes and stopped after 8 because accuracy had levelled off (~32 min on an M1 Pro GPU). Weights: models/cube_pose_synthetic.pt. Run files: args.yaml · results.csv.

Caveat

Every image here is synthetic. These scores show the model learned the task on computer-made frames; it still needs fine-tuning and testing on real photos of our cube.
