Which point goes where

  • 0 is the corner closest to the camera, where Top, Front and Right meet.
  • Going round: 1 top-left, 2 top (back), 3 top-right, 4 bottom-right, 5 bottom, 6 bottom-left.
  • "Top" is whichever side faces up in the picture. Put points on the cube's outer corners, even if a finger covers one: guess where it is.
  • If you can't see three sides of the cube, choose No cube here or delete the frame.
